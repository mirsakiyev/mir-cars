Before you book

Booking requirements and rental rules.

Driver requirements

Minimum driver age 25. Valid driver license, proof of insurance, and matching payment method are required.

Deposit and payment

Refundable deposit or card authorization is confirmed after approval. Stripe and Zelle options are available.

Mileage

Daily rentals include 150 miles. Additional mileage is billed after return at the listed overage rate.

Pickup and delivery

LAX, Glendale pickup, and custom delivery requests are reviewed with your rental details before confirmation.

Vehicle care

No smoking, racing, off-road use, pets without approval, or unauthorized drivers. Fees may apply.

Changes

Extensions, late returns, delivery changes, and vehicle swaps must be approved by MIR CARS before the trip changes.

At pickup

Bring your license, proof of insurance, payment method, and any required deposit confirmation.
