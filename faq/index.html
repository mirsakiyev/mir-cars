FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

F.A.Q.

Answers to common questions about reservations, payments, pickup, delivery, and vehicle rentals.

Booking

How do I reserve a vehicle?

Start on the booking page, choose your trip details, upload required information, and submit the request for review.

Can I choose a specific vehicle?

You can request a preferred vehicle. MIR CARS will confirm availability before the reservation is finalized.

Is vehicle availability guaranteed?

Availability is not guaranteed until MIR CARS confirms the reservation and any required payment or deposit steps are complete.

Payments & Deposits

When do I pay?

Payment timing depends on the confirmed reservation details and the payment instructions provided by MIR CARS.

Is a deposit required?

A refundable deposit or authorization may be required. Amounts vary by vehicle class and rental details.

What payment methods are accepted?

The checkout flow supports the payment methods currently offered by MIR CARS. Final instructions are provided during confirmation.

Pickup & Delivery

Where can I pick up the vehicle?

Pickup options are shown in the booking flow and may vary by vehicle, timing, and operational availability.

Can MIR CARS deliver the vehicle?

Delivery may be available for supported locations. The booking flow can collect custom delivery requests for review.

How are custom delivery fees calculated?

Custom delivery fees may depend on distance, service area, timing, and the final pickup or return plan.

Driver Requirements

What documents do I need?

A valid driver's license, insurance information, and matching reservation details may be required.

Do I need a valid driver's license?

Yes. A valid driver's license is required before a rental can be approved.

Can someone else drive the vehicle?

Only drivers approved by MIR CARS and listed as authorized drivers may operate the vehicle.

Vehicles

Are vehicles cleaned before rental?

MIR CARS prepares vehicles before pickup or delivery, subject to timing and operational needs.

Are electric vehicles charged before pickup?

Electric vehicle charge level may vary. MIR CARS will provide details during reservation confirmation.

Lost & Found

What should I do if I lost something in a vehicle?

Submit a Lost & Found report with your trip identifier, item description, and last known location.

How long does MIR CARS keep found items?

Retention may vary by item type and operational policy. MIR CARS will contact you if the item is located.
